# Spartan Radar

> Spartan Radar is an automotive radar software and sensing company, operating as the
> radar technology brand of Pro-Vision Solutions, LLC after its 2025 acquisition. It
> improves the resolution and reliability of existing automotive radar sensors through
> proprietary digital signal processing and machine-learning perception, and ships a
> software-defined collision-avoidance system for commercial and heavy vehicles.

## What Spartan Radar sells

- **Clarify** — radar perception software that upgrades legacy and current automotive
  radar with 3-8x resolution improvement, running on existing silicon (ARM Cortex,
  Texas Instruments TDA4VM and AWR series, NVIDIA Orin and DRIVE, NXP SAF85xx and
  SAF86xx radar SoCs), at the edge or on a central CPU/GPU. No additional hardware.
- **Hoplo** — a fully configurable, software-defined collision-avoidance and blind-spot
  warning system for commercial vehicles: mining, construction, material handling and
  logistics fleets.

Buyers are commercial-vehicle OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers and fleet operators. Delivery is
embedded software and hardware under bilateral commercial agreements, not self-service.
